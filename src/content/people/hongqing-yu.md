Hongqing Yu is an Associate Professor and MSc programme leader in the School of Computing at the University of Derby. His research in data science and machine learning addresses applications in industrial automation, digital twins, and healthcare.

His interests include explainable AI, natural language processing, and context-aware machine learning for systems that use data from connected devices.
