Jenny Clementson is a senior manager at the University of Derby’s Institute for Innovation in Sustainable Engineering and a Chartered Engineer. Her background spans engineering consultancy, railway systems, and independent safety assessment.

Her research examines how organisations can manage legal risk when using digital twins in regulated industries. Her interests also include model-based systems engineering, assurance, and safety.
