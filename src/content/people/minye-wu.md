Minye Wu is a Lecturer at the University of Derby. His research focuses on computer vision and 3D scene representations, including 3D reconstruction, novel view synthesis, and volumetric video.

He previously conducted postdoctoral research in the VISICS lab at KU Leuven. He received his PhD in 2021 from the University of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, with ShanghaiTech University as his primary training institution.
