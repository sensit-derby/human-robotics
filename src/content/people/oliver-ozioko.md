Oliver Ozioko is a Lecturer in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at the University of Derby. He designs and integrates sensors and electronic devices for robots, wearables, and healthcare applications.

His research includes electronic skin and haptic interfaces for human–machine interaction, self-powered wearable electronics, and assistive devices. He also explores how to integrate sensors and actuators into 3D-printed devices.
