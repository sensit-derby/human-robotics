Oluwarotimi Samuel is a Senior Lecturer in Computer Science at the University of Derby. He develops AI and machine learning methods for assistive and rehabilitation technologies, human–computer interaction, and other cyber-physical systems.

His research includes analysing muscle signals to infer movement intent for prosthetic control and rehabilitation. He holds a PhD in Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Systems from the University of the Chinese Academy of Sciences.
