Quang Dan Le is a Lecturer in Embedded Systems at the University of Derby. His research covers human–robot interaction, wearable haptics for teleoperation, force control, and fault-tolerant control for robot manipulators. He also studies reinforcement and imitation learning for robotics.

He received his PhD from the University of Ulsan, worked as a research scientist at A*STAR in Singapore, and conducted postdoctoral research at the University of Strathclyde on teleoperation and wearable haptics.
